# Marlboro Youth Flag League: the rules that shape our plays

This is a summary of the league's rule sheets (2025 Fall rules, grades 2-8), keeping what matters when designing and calling plays. When in doubt, the league's own document and the referee decide.

## The game

- **Players:** 7 vs 7. A team may play with 6 or 5; 4 or fewer is a forfeit.
- **Field:** 25 yards wide and 70 yards long, with 10-yard end zones.
- **Downs:** a drive starts at your own 5-yard line. You get 3 downs to cross midfield, then 4 downs to score. On 4th down, punt or go for it.
- **Clock:** 30 seconds to snap, huddle included. Two 24-minute halves on a running clock. 2 timeouts per half.

## Scoring

- **Touchdown:** 6 points.
- **Extra point:** 1 point from the 5-yard line, **pass only**; or 2 points from the 10, run or pass. Declare which before the try.
- **Safety:** 2 points. An intercepted extra-point try can be returned for 2.

## Running

- **One run play per four downs.** A handoff that ends in a pass is a pass play.
- **No-run zones:** the 5 yards before midfield and the 5 yards before the end zone. Every play there must be a pass, even after a handoff, and the defense can't blitz there.
- **QB runs:** the QB can't run unless he's being blitzed.
- **Handoffs:** allowed behind the line, including several on one play. The first handoff can't go to the center (no "center sneak").
- **Laterals and throws:** laterals are allowed behind the line. Anyone who got a handoff may throw from behind the line. A pass or lateral after crossing the line is illegal.
- **Rushing after a handoff:** once the ball is handed off, every defender may rush.
- **No blocking or screening.** Teammates without the ball must stop moving once the ball crosses the line; no running alongside the runner.

## Passing and receiving

- **7 seconds:** the QB has 7 seconds to throw, or the play is dead.
- **Throwaways** must get past the line of scrimmage, or it's intentional grounding.
- **Passes:** one forward pass per play, from behind the line. Shovel passes are allowed.
- **Eligible receivers:** everyone, including the QB and the center. A catch needs the first foot inbounds.
- **Motion:** one player at a time, moving sideways and never toward the line.
- **Formation:** 1 to 5 players on the line. The QB must be off the line.

## Defense and flags

- **The blitz:** only 1 defender may rush, **once every 4 downs**, from 7 yards back at the snap. No blitz in the no-run zones, and no contact with the QB.
- **Down:** a runner is down when his flag is pulled or falls off, his knee or arm touches, or he steps out of bounds. There are no fumbles.
- **Flag guarding** (stiff-arming, covering the flag, spinning out of control) costs 10 yards and a down.

## What this means for our plays

1. **Time to throw:** on 3 of every 4 downs nobody rushes the QB, so he has up to 7 seconds. Patient routes and double moves work.
2. **Extra defender:** the defense can drop all 7 against our 6 receivers, so one defender is always free. We beat that by putting 3 receivers against 2 defenders on one side, and by making the QB read one defender who has to choose (blue = first look, green = second).
3. **The run is precious:** we get one per 4 downs. Notes on run plays start with "Your run:".
4. **Near the goal line** and on the 1-point try, we need our best passes, like Spacing and Stick.
5. **The blitz down** needs a quick throw, like Sprint Out or Hot Potato. It's the only time the QB may run.
6. **Legal sideways motion** shows man-to-man (a defender follows) versus zone.
7. **The stopwatches** on each card show how long the play takes. 1 means the ball comes out fast, which is right for the blitz down. 3 means the play needs time to open up, so save it for a down with no rusher.
